# Privacy Policy for TrackPe

Last updated: 2026-05-05

TrackPe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect

- Transaction details that you add or import (for example: amount, date, category, and merchant text).
- App preferences and settings.
- Optional files you choose to import (for example, statement PDFs) for processing.

## How We Use Information

- To provide expense tracking and categorization features.
- To generate summaries, insights, and reminders inside the app.
- To improve app stability and user experience.

## Data Storage

- Your data is primarily stored locally on your device.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Data Sharing

- We do not share your personal financial data with third parties for advertising.
- We may use trusted service libraries required for core app features (for example OCR/text processing).

## Your Choices

- You can remove app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
- You can revoke permissions in Android settings at any time.

## Children's Privacy
