# Privacy Policy for Think Twice: Fun Brain Puzzles

Last updated: 2026-10-19

This policy explains how Think Twice: Fun Brain Puzzles ("the app") handles your information.

## Information We Handle

The app does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment details.

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- Puzzle progress and completed levels.
- Dates on which you played, for local activity summaries.
- App preferences, including reduced motion and vibration settings.
- Onboarding status and saved game state.

We do not receive this gameplay information on our servers. The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or in-app purchases.

Illustrated camera and photo puzzles do not access your device's camera or personal photos.

## How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to remember your progress, display your activity, and apply your preferences.

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond and provide support. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver the email service.

## Storage and Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and relies on your operating system's storage protections.

Your operating system may include app data in backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. We do not receive or control these backups.

App stores and backup providers handle information under their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not send your saved gameplay information to advertisers or analytics providers.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider. We may disclose correspondence if legally required.

## Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

On Android, you can remove saved progress and preferences through:

Settings → Apps → Think Twice → Storage → Clear storage

Uninstalling removes the app's local data, but backup copies may remain or be restored later. Manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider's settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us.

There is no app account to delete.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not request personal information from children and does not include account registration, chat, or advertising.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support enquiry, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.

If advertising, analytics, or purchase features are introduced, we will update the relevant disclosures before releasing those features.
